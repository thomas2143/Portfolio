Meridian CA — A Grounded RAG
Assistant with a Visible Retrieval Trace

The problem A pre-sales assistant over a martech vendor's knowledge base. Most RAG demos fail silently on one case: a question the base does not cover retrieves a chunk that sounds related, and the model answers anyway. This system makes refusal a first-class, verifiable outcome.

The approach Three permitted behaviours instead of two — answer when a chunk holds the fact, escalate when the boundary is documented but the fact is not public (pricing, SLAs), refuse when nothing covers it. Every claim is traced to a cited chunk, and the interface shows all five retrieval candidates with their distances, marking which were dropped.
